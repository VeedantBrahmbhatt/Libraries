# Evaluation phase record

## 2026-11-15: pilot evaluation framework

The published 91,667-book index is pinned to Hugging Face revision `f54ded49b97a92ca8e5dce0463500043ea2a19a4`. We chose a reproducible cloud pilot because the team wants to own the candidate selection and ranking while keeping the full index off laptops.

We added 12 authored questions across English, Spanish, French, and German. They include subject searches and one known-title query. Development and holdout labels are included so later ranking changes can be checked without using every question for tuning. This set checks the machinery and exposes examples; it does not represent actual student needs.

The collector runs keyword, semantic, and hybrid modes on every question, times warm searches, and pools the top 10 candidates for human review. The review sheet includes metadata and a short abstract excerpt; relevance is deliberately blank. Checksums and source revisions make the output traceable. The scorer rejects incomplete, invalid, duplicated, or changed judgments before reporting Success@5, pooled nDCG@10, known-title top-one, warm p95 latency, group breakdowns, and abstract-availability counts.

The GitHub Actions job uses temporary runner storage for the pinned index and model. Its browser summary contains the review pool, and its short-lived artifact contains the CSV files. No third-party search API selects candidates. The separate [runbook](runbook.md) covers operation and grading.

The [first cloud evaluation run](https://github.com/VeedantBrahmbhatt/Libraries/actions/runs/43693270526) passed on 2026-11-15. It collected 233 distinct question–book pairs from 12 questions, left every relevance grade blank, and deliberately skipped scoring. The [five-file review artifact](https://github.com/VeedantBrahmbhatt/Libraries/actions/runs/43693270526/artifacts/14153335931) was uploaded successfully at 113,795 bytes and expires on 2026-12-15. The job's browser summary shows all review candidates without a laptop index download. These are operational results only; they do not establish that any returned book is useful.

Changing `pyproject.toml` also triggered the existing [DOAB preparation workflow](https://github.com/VeedantBrahmbhatt/Libraries/actions/runs/43693270595). ## 2026-11-15: AI review and answer-derived search diagnosis

The user asked for new questions written backward from returned books, an assessment of the model's responses, and comparison with the provisional KPI bars. We kept two measurements separate. The [diagnostic design](answer-derived-design.md) and [implementation plan](answer-derived-plan.md) were recorded before scoring. The purpose branch `diagnose-answer-derived-search` contains the work; no branch was given a `codex` name.

We took only the 114 KB pilot ZIP from the [verified handoff run](https://github.com/VeedantBrahmbhatt/Libraries/actions/runs/43694776677). We checked the review checksum. The assistant then assigned `0`, `1`, or `2` to all 233 saved question–book pairs from title, subjects, language, and at most 600 characters of abstract. This produced 77 unsupported, 97 partial, and 59 clearly useful grades. The [AI grade file](../../evaluation/ai-review/pilot-grades.csv) is separate from the 12 blank human-review files. `diagnostic.py` rejects missing, extra, duplicated, and invalid grades before invoking the existing score validator. The resulting [pilot metrics](../../evaluation/ai-review/pilot-metrics.json) show top-five success of 7/12 keyword, 11/12 semantic, and 10/12 hybrid. Hybrid nDCG is 53.0% over keyword, while semantic nDCG is 21.6% over hybrid. The only known-title question ranked first in all modes. These are small AI-judged pilot results, not human KPI evidence.

We selected two distinct source books per pilot question by a fixed SHA-256 ordering, requiring a usable abstract and HTTPS source. We verified all 24 book IDs against the saved review pool. We wrote paraphrased questions in English, Spanish, French, and German, then committed the [frozen queries](../../evaluation/queries/answer-derived.jsonl) and [target map](../../evaluation/queries/answer-derived-targets.json) before starting the new cloud run. No full dataset or index was downloaded to the laptop. Synthetic tests went red before implementation, then the full local suite passed **45 tests** with Ruff clean.

The branch was pushed and fast-forwarded to `main` to trigger the new workflow. The [answer-derived run](https://github.com/VeedantBrahmbhatt/Libraries/actions/runs/43775201104), [code checks](https://github.com/VeedantBrahmbhatt/Libraries/actions/runs/43775201110), and existing [evaluation run](https://github.com/VeedantBrahmbhatt/Libraries/actions/runs/43775201145) passed. The [small diagnostic artifact](https://github.com/VeedantBrahmbhatt/Libraries/actions/runs/43775201104/artifacts/14186206397) expires on 2026-12-15. Its manifest pins the 91,667-book index revision and exact question hash. We recomputed the cloud `target-metrics.json` locally from the small artifact and got an exact match. The [saved metrics](../../evaluation/diagnostics/answer-derived-metrics.json) show source-book recovery in the top five for 17/24 keyword, 23/24 semantic, and 20/24 hybrid. Three semantic rank-2-or-3 target books vanished from hybrid's top ten.

The [results report](answer-derived-results.md) gives the score comparisons and weak cases. Our judgment is that the current semantic mode is the stronger prototype baseline and the existing fusion should be tested with more human-reviewed queries before choosing it as default. The 24 questions are answer-derived and all development cases; they cannot prove general student usefulness. No natural student questions or human relevance grades were added.

## 2026-11-15: fifty fictional student searches

The user asked for 50 different undergraduate and graduate personas, queries in their own words, actual searches, book-fit judgments, and a documented comparison of ranking modes. We used the purpose branch `evaluate-50-student-personas`. The [design](persona-design.md) and [plan](persona-plan.md) fixed the comparison before the real search. We wrote 25 undergraduate and 25 graduate profiles across 50 different programs. Forty queries are English, four Spanish, four French, and two German. Each profile gives a learning goal and reading need, including some explicit beginner or advanced constraints. The profiles contain no real student information. The query export was tested and frozen in Git before seeing results; its SHA-256 is `2c076f2ab46b031f054cfb0938b0b70c014a2aca148a5095da4804af55fe84ba`.

The new [GitHub Actions run](https://github.com/VeedantBrahmbhatt/Libraries/actions/runs/43776657495) passed. It checked that the profile and query files agreed, downloaded the existing pinned 91,667-book index to a temporary runner, and searched keyword, semantic, and hybrid top-ten results. The validated manifest reports **1,113 distinct question–book pairs**. Its 605 KB ZIP was saved to [the persona folder](../../evaluation/personas/README.md) so the team can inspect or rescore it after GitHub's 30-day artifact expires. No full dataset, model cache, or index was saved on the laptop.

We reviewed the union of all top-five results, **599 distinct pairs**, from title, subjects, language, and at most 600 characters of abstract. The separate [assistant labels](../../evaluation/personas/ai-labels.csv) contain 199 off-topic/unusable, 359 partial/uncertain, and 41 clearly suitable grades. We audited four initially strong grades and reduced them to partial because the books missed a specific part of the request. Among 129 strong topic matches, 54 had unclear reading-level evidence and 34 visibly conflicted with it. The scorer requires one valid grade for every top-five pair and rejects changed run files or questions. The [results report](persona-results.md) and [50-row findings](../../evaluation/personas/persona-findings.csv) preserve the judgments and example books.

The scored top-five success is **11/50 keyword, 14/50 semantic, and 15/50 hybrid**. Hybrid has the highest pooled nDCG@5 (0.7448), semantic has the most clearly suitable first results (8/50) and lowest warm p95 (54 ms), and keyword is weaker. Hybrid leads for the 25 undergraduate cases (9 successes versus semantic's 6), while semantic leads for the 25 graduate cases (8 versus hybrid's 6). No mode found a clear fit for any of the four Spanish or four French scenarios; these groups are small and need direct corpus and ranking review. The provisional 80% top-five target is not reached numerically in this authored study. It cannot validate nDCG@10 or known-title KPIs.

The previous answer-derived test favored semantic, while this more varied persona test gives hybrid a narrow top-five edge. We therefore do not choose a dependable default from these two assistant-created studies. Future work should get natural anonymous student questions, human judgments, and full-book checks for uncertain level claims; then test any ranking change on held-out questions. The existing human pilot sheets still have no human grades.
